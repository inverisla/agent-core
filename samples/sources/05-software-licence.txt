# SOFTWARE SUBSCRIPTION AND SUPPORT AGREEMENT

This agreement is between Tidewell Software Ltd ("Tidewell") and Penhallow Veterinary Group Ltd ("the Customer").

## 1. Subscription

Tidewell grants the Customer a subscription to the Tidewell Practice Manager software for up to 120 named users, with standard support during UK business hours.

## 2. Subscription period

The first subscription period is 12 months from 1 May 2026.

## 3. Automatic renewal

The subscription renews automatically for a further 12 months at the end of each subscription period.

## 4. Cancelling

To stop the subscription renewing, either party must give written notice at least 60 days before the renewal date.

## 5. Fees

The subscription fee is £18,000 a year, payable annually in advance on the start of each subscription period. Tidewell may increase the fee by up to 3% at each renewal by telling the Customer at least 90 days before the renewal date. Payment is due within 30 days of invoice.

## 6. Liability

Tidewell's total liability in any subscription period is limited to the fees paid in that period.
