# CONTRACT FOR CLEANING SERVICES

Between Sparrowgate Cleaning Co. Ltd (the Contractor) and The Kiln Studios Ltd (the Client).

1. The Contractor will clean the Client's premises at Unit 3, Kiln Yard, Sampleford, five evenings a week, Monday to Friday, to the standard set out in the cleaning schedule.

2. This contract starts on 6 October 2025 and runs from month to month until either party ends it.

3. Either party may end this contract by giving one calendar month's notice in writing.

4. The Client will pay £2,350 per month plus VAT. The Contractor will invoice at the end of each month and the Client will pay within 21 days. Extra cleaning outside the schedule is charged at £24 per hour.

5. The Contractor will provide all cleaning materials and equipment.

6. The Contractor will hold public liability insurance of at least £5 million.
