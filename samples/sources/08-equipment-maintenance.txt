# EQUIPMENT RENTAL AND MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT

The parties are Voltaire Medtech B.V., of Amsterdam, the Netherlands (the "Owner"), and Ashcombe Diagnostics Ltd, of Sampleford, England (the "Hirer").

## 1. Equipment

The Owner rents to the Hirer two laboratory analysers, model VX-400, and will maintain them, including parts, labour and an annual calibration.

## 2. Rental period

The minimum rental period is five years from the date the equipment is installed, which is expected to be 2 March 2026.

## 3. After the minimum period

After the minimum rental period the agreement continues from year to year.

## 4. Notice

Either party may end the agreement at the end of the minimum rental period, or at the end of any later year, by giving at least six months' written notice.

## 5. Rental and maintenance charges

The Hirer will pay €9,600 per quarter, in advance, for rental and maintenance of both analysers. Call-outs outside working hours cost €180 each. Invoices are payable within 45 days.

## 6. Risk

The equipment remains the Owner's property. The Hirer must insure it against loss and damage while it is at the Hirer's premises.
