# SUPPLY AGREEMENT FOR OFFICE CONSUMABLES

This agreement is made between Fernhill Office Supplies Ltd, a company registered in England, of 12 Quarry Lane, Exampleton (the "Supplier"), and Larkspur Housing Trust, of 4 Mill Street, Sampleford (the "Customer").

## 1. Supply

1.1 The Supplier will supply paper, toner, stationery and other office consumables listed in its catalogue, as the Customer orders them from time to time.

1.2 Orders may be placed online or by email. The Supplier will deliver within two working days of an order.

## 2. Term

2.1 This agreement starts on 1 April 2026 and continues for an initial term of two years.

## 3. Renewal

3.1 At the end of the initial term this agreement renews automatically for successive periods of 12 months, unless either party gives notice under clause 4.

## 4. Notice

4.1 Either party may stop the agreement renewing by giving the other at least 90 days' written notice before the end of the initial term or of any renewal period.

## 5. Prices and payment

5.1 Goods are charged at the prices in the Supplier's catalogue on the date of the order, less a discount of 12%.

5.2 The Supplier will invoice monthly in arrears. The Customer will pay each invoice within 30 days of receiving it.

5.3 Delivery is free for orders over £75. Smaller orders carry a delivery charge of £6.50.

## 6. General

6.1 This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.

Signed for Fernhill Office Supplies Ltd and for Larkspur Housing Trust.
