# MARKETING SERVICES RETAINER

Between Copperleaf Creative Ltd (the "Agency") and Westerby Cycles Ltd (the "Client").

1. Services. The Agency will plan and run the Client's online marketing, including social media, search advertising and a monthly newsletter, as set out in the agreed plan.

2. Start and duration. This agreement starts on the date it is signed by both parties and lasts for 18 months.

3. Extension. The parties may extend this agreement by agreeing in writing before it ends.

4. Ending the agreement. Either party may end this agreement by giving three months' written notice.

5. Fees. The Client will pay a retainer of £6,000 per month, invoiced monthly in advance, plus the cost of advertising space bought for the Client, which the Agency will pass on at cost. Reasonable expenses agreed in advance are also payable. Invoices are due within 30 days.

6. Ownership. The Client owns the materials the Agency creates for it once the relevant fees are paid.
