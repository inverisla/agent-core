# WAREHOUSING AND DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT

Made between Granitebeck Logistics Ltd ("Granitebeck") and Mayweather Garden Goods Ltd ("the Customer").

## 1. Services

Granitebeck will store the Customer's goods at its warehouse in Exampleton and deliver them to the Customer's retail customers as instructed.

## 2. Term

This agreement starts on 1 February 2026 and ends on 31 December 2028.

## 3. Charges

| Charge | Rate |
| Storage | £4.20 per pallet per week |
| Pick and pack | £1.15 per order line |
| Next-day delivery | £7.90 per parcel |
| Minimum monthly charge | £3,500 |

Granitebeck will invoice weekly. The Customer will pay within 30 days of the invoice date.

## 4. Insurance

Granitebeck will insure the goods in its care for their replacement value.

## 5. Liability for goods

Granitebeck is liable for loss of or damage to the goods while in its care, up to £100 per pallet unless the loss is caused by its negligence.
