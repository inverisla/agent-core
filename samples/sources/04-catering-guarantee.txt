# CATERING SUPPLY AGREEMENT

THIS AGREEMENT is between:

(1) Hollowmere Fresh Foods Ltd (the "Supplier");

(2) Quillfield Academy Trust (the "Customer"); and

(3) Hollowmere Group Holdings plc (the "Guarantor"), the Supplier's parent company.

## 1. Supply

The Supplier will supply prepared school meals to the Customer's four schools on every school day in term time.

## 2. Term

The agreement runs for three years from 1 September 2026.

## 3. Extension

The Customer may extend the agreement once, for a further two years, by giving the Supplier at least six months' written notice before the end of the initial three years. There is no other renewal.

## 4. Termination

The Customer may end the agreement with three months' written notice if the Supplier fails two food hygiene inspections in any 12 month period. Otherwise neither party may end the agreement early, except for material breach.

## 5. Price

The Customer will pay £2.85 for each primary meal and £3.40 for each secondary meal served, invoiced every four weeks and payable within 30 days. Prices are fixed for the first year and then reviewed each September by agreement.

## 6. Guarantee

The Guarantor guarantees the Supplier's performance of this agreement and will make good any loss the Customer suffers if the Supplier fails to perform.
