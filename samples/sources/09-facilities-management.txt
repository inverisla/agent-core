# FACILITIES MANAGEMENT SERVICES AGREEMENT

## PARTIES

(1) Ravensholt Facilities Ltd, a company registered in England with number 00000001 (the "Service Provider").

(2) The Board of Trustees of Example Museum of Industry (the "Authority").

## BACKGROUND

The Authority wishes to appoint a service provider for the maintenance, cleaning and security of its museum buildings, and the Service Provider has agreed to provide these services on the terms of this agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 In this agreement:

"Commencement Date" means 1 July 2026;

"Contract Year" means each period of 12 months starting on the Commencement Date or on an anniversary of it;

"Initial Term" means the period of four years starting on the Commencement Date;

"Services" means the services described in Schedule 1;

"Service Charges" means the charges set out in Schedule 2.

## 2. TERM

2.1 This agreement starts on the Commencement Date and continues for the Initial Term, unless it is extended under clause 2.2 or ended earlier under clause 12.

2.2 The Authority may extend this agreement twice, each time for a further period of 12 months, by giving the Service Provider at least three months' written notice before the end of the Initial Term or of the first extension.

## 3. THE SERVICES

3.1 The Service Provider will provide the Services from the Commencement Date to the standards in Schedule 1.

3.2 The Service Provider will provide enough suitably trained staff to perform the Services, and will vet all staff who work in the museum's collections areas.

3.3 The Service Provider will report on its performance each month against the service levels in Schedule 1.

## 4. AUTHORITY'S OBLIGATIONS

4.1 The Authority will give the Service Provider's staff reasonable access to the buildings and will provide a store room and a staff room free of charge.

## 5. SERVICE CHARGES AND PAYMENT

5.1 In return for the Services the Authority will pay the Service Charges in Schedule 2.

5.2 The Service Provider will invoice monthly in arrears. The Authority will pay valid invoices within 30 days.

5.3 The Service Charges will be adjusted on the first day of each Contract Year after the first, by the change in the Retail Prices Index over the previous 12 months.

## 6. SERVICE CREDITS

6.1 If the Service Provider fails to meet a service level, the Authority will receive the service credit set out in Schedule 1, deducted from the next invoice. Service credits will not exceed 10% of the Service Charges in any month.

## 7. INSURANCE

7.1 The Service Provider will maintain public liability insurance of at least £10 million and employer's liability insurance as required by law.

## 8. CONFIDENTIALITY

8.1 Each party will keep the other's confidential information secret and use it only for this agreement.

## 9. DATA PROTECTION

9.1 The Service Provider will process personal data only on the Authority's written instructions.

## 10. LIABILITY

10.1 Neither party limits its liability for death or personal injury caused by its negligence.

10.2 Subject to clause 10.1, each party's total liability in each Contract Year is limited to 125% of the Service Charges paid or payable in that Contract Year.

## 11. CHANGES

11.1 Either party may propose a change to the Services. No change takes effect until both parties agree it in writing.

## 12. TERMINATION

12.1 The Authority may end this agreement at any time, without giving a reason, by giving the Service Provider at least six months' written notice.

12.2 Either party may end this agreement at once by written notice if the other commits a material breach that cannot be remedied, or does not remedy a breach within 30 days of being asked to.

## 13. GOVERNING LAW

13.1 This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.

## SCHEDULE 1: SERVICES AND SERVICE LEVELS

Planned maintenance of heating, lighting, lifts and fire systems; reactive repairs, with urgent faults attended within 4 hours; daily cleaning of public areas before opening; security staff on site 24 hours a day.

## SCHEDULE 2: SERVICE CHARGES

| Service | Annual charge |
| Planned and reactive maintenance | £148,000 |
| Cleaning | £96,500 |
| Security | £212,000 |
| Total | £456,500 |

The annual charges are payable in 12 equal monthly instalments.
